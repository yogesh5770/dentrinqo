1. Hardcoded Fallback Production Secret in Patient Auth
File: apps/api/src/patient-auth/patient-auth.service.ts
Line: 44
Code snippet:

2. PatientAuthGuard Lacks Cross-Tenant Host Validation
File: apps/api/src/patient-auth/guards/patient-auth.guard.ts
Lines: 20-35
Observation: Validates JWT signature and extracts payload.clinicId, but NEVER compares payload.clinicId against the request host's req.clinicId. A valid patient token from Clinic A can be passed to Clinic B endpoints.

3. Missing Account Lockout & Brute-Force Rate Limiting for Staff Login
File: apps/api/src/auth/auth.service.ts (lines 77-128) & apps/api/src/auth/auth.controller.ts (lines 20-40)
Observation: No @Throttle decorator on staff login endpoint. Staff accounts lack failedLoginAttempts and lockoutUntil fields (unlike PatientAccount). Brute-force dictionary attacks against staff accounts are unimpeded.
