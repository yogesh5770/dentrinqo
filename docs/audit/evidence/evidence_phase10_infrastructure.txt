4. Lack of Single-Tenant Backup & Restore Capability
File: deploy_vps_ubuntu.sh (lines 80-95) & scripts/backup_postgresql.sh
Observation: Backup script executes pg_dump on the entire database (all clinics combined). There is no provision to backup or restore a single tenant clinic independently. Restoring a backup for Clinic A overwrites all data for Clinic B.
